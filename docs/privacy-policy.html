Privacy Policy — 2048

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy explains what the 2048 Android app (package com.kriruo.game2048) collects, why, and what control you have over it. It is a single-player puzzle game made by an independent developer. There are no accounts, no advertising, and nothing is sold to anyone.

The short version

  • The game asks, on first launch, whether you're willing to share anonymous usage and crash data.
  • If you say no, nothing is sent. The game works exactly the same.
  • If you say yes, anonymous data about how the game is played and any crashes is sent to Google Firebase.
  • You can change your answer at any time, in the game, under Your Stats.
  • No account, name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or payment details are ever collected.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress — current board, best score, level, daily streak, chosen theme and board size, and lifetime statistics — is saved only on your own device. It is never uploaded. If you uninstall the game, it is gone.

What is sent, if you agree

Only if you choose "Allow", the game uses two Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous events describing how the game is played: starting a game, reaching a new level, a game ending, unlocking a theme or board size, using an in-game Joker, completing the Daily Challenge. Google also automatically records technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, approximate country and a randomly generated app-instance identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — if the app crashes, a report containing the technical stack trace, device model, operating system version and app version.

This is used for one purpose: working out which parts of the game are broken or unreachable, so they can be fixed. It is never used to identify you, and it is not shared with advertisers or sold.

What is never collected

No name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone or camera input, precise location, or payment information. The app requests no Android permissions for any of these. There is no sign-in and no user account of any kind.

Children

This game is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13 in particular. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included. If you are a parent and have a question about this, please get in touch using the address below.

Who processes the data

Anonymous analytics and crash data are processed by Google as part of Firebase. Their handling is covered by Firebase's privacy documentation and Google's privacy policy. Data may be processed on Google servers outside your country. No other third party receives any data from this app.

How long it is kept

Analytics data is retained according to the Firebase project's retention setting, after which Google deletes it automatically. Crash reports are retained for up to 90 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn it off at any time — in the game, open Your Stats and switch off "Share anonymous data". Collection stops immediately.
  • Delete everything — uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress. To request deletion of previously collected anonymous data, contact the address below; because the data holds no identifier tied to you personally, please include the approximate dates and device model so it can be located.
  • If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, the legal basis for this processing is your consent, which you give on first launch and may withdraw at any time as described above.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes affecting data already collected will be accompanied by a fresh in-app request.
